## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🛡️ Prahari: Crowd Flow Intelligence
**Real-time crowd flow intelligence to prevent stadium stampedes, without cameras.**

Venues track total headcount, but that doesn't show *which* corridor or stairwell is about to become dangerous. Prahari estimates crowd density and flow **zone by zone** using signals from phones people already carry (Wi-Fi access-point counts and passive probe-request rates), then feeds them into an **explainable risk engine**. It flags *where* flow is failing, *why*, and *how long* until it turns critical. A human operator always confirms the response, so the system recommends and simulates but never acts on its own.

**Highlights:**

- 📡 Camera-free sensing, so it still works in smoke, darkness and panic
- 🗺️ Zone-level density & flow monitoring
- 🧠 Explainable risk engine that shows its reasoning
- 🎯 Targeted response: opens the *one* alternate route that's needed, not every gate
- 🖥️ Interactive dark-themed dashboard for operators
- 🏆 Built for a hackathon

**Tech:** `Python` `FastAPI` `scikit-learn` `React` `Vite` `Tailwind CSS` `WebSockets`

🔗 **Live Demo:** [prahari-crowd-management-intelligen.vercel.app](https://prahari-crowd-management-intelligen.vercel.app/)
📂 **Code:** [Prahari_crowd_management_intelligence](https://github.com/CodeWithArnav198-wpr/Prahari_crowd_management_intelligence)

### 👓 Vigilant Vision: Anti-Sleep Smart Glasses
**Hardware that keeps drivers awake and roads safer.**

Drowsy driving is dangerous because it strikes without warning. Vigilant Vision is a pair of smart glasses that watches for signs of a driver nodding off. If the driver's eyes stay closed too long or they seem dizzy, the glasses respond right away with a **buzzer** and a **vibration motor**, giving the driver a physical wake-up call before it's too late.

**Highlights:**

- 👁️ Detects prolonged eye closure
- 😵 Alerts on signs of dizziness or drowsiness
- 🔔 Instant dual alert: buzzer (sound) + vibration motor (touch)
- 🧠 Arduino-based control logic
- 🪶 Wearable, compact and hands-free
- 🚗 Built to prevent drowsy-driving accidents

**Tech:** `Arduino` `C/C++` `Python`
